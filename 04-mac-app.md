# 4 - Mac app

The Mac app shows your spaces and lets you read, write and organise them. It talks to Knode Service,
so you see what your agents write as it happens.

> Not released yet: a first version arrives with v0.1.

## In v0.1

- Open a space.
- List its nodes.
- Read and edit a note in Markdown, with a live preview.
- Search.

## Next

- Several spaces, switched like Apple Music libraries (v0.2).
- History of every change, and who made it (v0.2).
- A map of your nodes and links (v0.3).

See the [roadmap](https://knode.md/plan) for the rest.
